Our History — A Living Tribute

From 6 boys in 1940 to one of Ghana's most respected schools.

22 January 1940: Founded by Rev. Dr. A.W.E. Appiah with only 6 boys. First named Aggrey Memorial College after his uncle, Dr. James Kwegyir Aggrey.

First Home: "Tandon Kuma", House No. 39, Jukwa Road, Cape Coast.

1947: A.M.E. Zion Church took over. Name changed to Aggrey Memorial A.M.E. Zion Secondary School.

January 1958: Moved to present 43-acre site at Brafo Yaw.

1997: Honoured by WAEC for academic excellence.

Today: Over 2,900 students.
